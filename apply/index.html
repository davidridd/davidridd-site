Ghostwriting

apply.

I take on a small number of ghostwriting projects each year so that every book gets the time it deserves. Right now there’s one spot left for 2026. If you want it, tell me about your book. I read every application myself and reply within two business days.

who this is for.

  • A story worth a bookYou’ve lived something, built something, or learned something a reader needs, and you can tell it in your own words. Not sure it’s enough? Apply anyway. That’s what the call is for.
  • Time to talkInterviews are the raw material. Plan on regular recorded conversations, in person or online, while we’re drafting.
  • A real timelineA book takes months, not weeks. If you have a hard date, a launch, an event, a season, say so and we’ll plan around it.
  • A budget from $15,000That’s the floor for a full-length book. The fee depends on length, research, and travel, and we agree on scope and a payment schedule before a word is written.

the application.

Six things. Short answers are fine; I’ll ask the rest on the call.

  1. Your name, and the best email to reach you.
  2. The book, in a paragraph. What it’s about, and the one thing a reader should walk away with.
  3. Who it’s for, and what you want it to do for you. The reader you picture, and the goal behind the book: clients, a speaking career, a legacy, a record for your family.
  4. Where you are. An idea, a pile of notes, a rough outline, a partial draft, or a finished manuscript that needs a rewrite.
  5. Why now. What changed, or what’s coming, that makes this the year.
  6. Timeline and budget. When you’d like to start, when you’d like a finished manuscript, and the range you’re working with.
