For ghostwriting clients

how a book gets written.

  1. Meet

    A free 30-minute call about your idea, your reader, and what you want the book to do for you.

  2. Interview

    Recorded conversations, in person or online, until I have your story and the way you tell it.

  3. Write

    An outline you approve, then chapters delivered as they’re finished and revised with you until they’re right.

  4. Publish

    Help choosing between traditional, hybrid, and self-publishing, then the pieces every finished book needs: cover copy, metadata, and a launch plan.

good questions.

Will anyone know you wrote my book?

Not unless you want them to. Ghostwriting is confidential by default. Credit, if any, is your call.

Who owns the manuscript?

You do. The words, the copyright, and the decisions about what happens next are yours.

Do you work with self-publishing authors?

Yes. I’ve worked on books published traditionally, through hybrid presses, and by the author, and I’ll help you choose the route that fits.

What if we’re not a fit?

Then I’ll say so on the first call, and point you toward what you need instead.
