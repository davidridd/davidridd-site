the book.

In Your Corner

Train Your Mind Like a Fighter to Face Life Like a Champion

Javier Mendez with David Ridd

Foreword by Khabib Nurmagomedov · Orbita Media

Javier Mendez founded the American Kickboxing Academy (AKA) in San José, California, coaching and cornering for MMA icons like Khabib Nurmagomedov, Islam Makhachev, Daniel Cormier, and Cain Velasquez. In Your Corner is part memoir, part mindset book that documents Javier’s life and lessons. His goal wasn’t to write a book about fighting, but what fighting taught him about pressure, preparation, and the people in his corner along the way. We built it together from hours of recorded interviews with Javier and the people closest to him, and we couldn’t be more excited to share these stories.
